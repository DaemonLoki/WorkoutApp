Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works on your iPhone and Apple Watch without one. Cloud Sync (Settings → Cloud Sync, Sign in with Apple) is optional and backs up your training log so it returns after a reinstall.

Why does the app ask for Apple Health access?

To show your heart rate and calories during a Session and to save each Session to Apple Health as a strength workout. This data stays on your devices. You can change access in the Health app under Sharing → Apps → OnlyWorkout.

How do I run a Session on my Apple Watch?

Open OnlyWorkout on the Watch and start the next Workout, or start it on the iPhone with "Start Sessions on Apple Watch" turned on in Settings. The Watch runs the whole Session on its own, even without the iPhone nearby, and sends it to the iPhone afterwards.

Strava says "Full for now". What does that mean?

Strava limits how many people can connect to an app until Strava has reviewed it, and every place is currently taken. Your Sessions stay in OnlyWorkout; check back later.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Cloud Sync → Delete Account deletes everything in the cloud, including the Strava connection. Deleting the app deletes the data on your devices. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
