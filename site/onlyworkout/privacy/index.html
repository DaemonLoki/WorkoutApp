OnlyWorkout

Privacy Policy

Effective 8 October 2026

OnlyWorkout is a strength-training tracker for iPhone and Apple Watch. It works without an account, shows no ads, contains no analytics or tracking, and never sells data. This policy explains what the app stores, what leaves your devices when you turn on the optional features, and how to delete it.

Who is responsible

OnlyWorkout is made by Stefan Blos, who is the controller for the data described here. Contact: stefan.blos@gmail.com.

On your devices

  • Your training log (Workouts, Exercises, Sessions, Sets with reps, weights and times, and progression suggestions) and your settings are stored on your iPhone and Apple Watch.
  • iPhone and Apple Watch exchange your plan and Sessions directly through Apple's Watch connection. This doesn't go through us.
  • Apple Health, if you allow it: during a Session the app reads your heart rate and active calories to show them, and saves each Session to Apple Health as a strength workout with its heart rate and calories. This Health data stays on your devices. It is never sent to us, to our cloud provider or to Strava, and it is never used for advertising. You can change Health access at any time in the Health app.

Deleting the app deletes this local data. Workouts saved to Apple Health stay in Apple Health until you delete them there.

Cloud Sync (optional)

If you sign in with Apple under Settings → Cloud Sync, the app backs up your training log so it returns after a reinstall or on a new iPhone.

  • What is stored: your Workouts, Exercises (including names you type for your own Exercises), Sessions, Sets and progression suggestions, and the account identifier Apple provides for Sign in with Apple. The app doesn't ask Apple for your email address.
  • Where: with Supabase, our hosting provider, in the European Union (Ireland). Supabase processes the data on our behalf and may process technical data such as your IP address when the app connects, to run and secure the service.
  • Why: only to back up and restore your training log for you (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Health data is never part of it.
  • Who can see it: only your account. Nobody else's app can read it.

Strava (optional)

If you connect Strava under Settings → Strava (Cloud Sync is required), the app uploads your finished Sessions to your Strava account.

  • What is sent per Session: the Workout name, start time and duration, and each Set's exercise type, reps and weight. Heart rate, calories and other Apple Health data are never sent.
  • What we keep: the access tokens Strava issues and your Strava athlete ID, stored with Supabase and only readable by our server functions, so that uploads work. The ID of each uploaded Strava activity stays on the iPhone that uploaded it and is deleted after 7 days. Your cloud data keeps the date each Session was uploaded, so it isn't uploaded twice.
  • Disconnecting: in Settings → Strava, or in your Strava settings. Either way the tokens are revoked and deleted.
  • Data on Strava is governed by Strava's Privacy Policy. Strava collects usage data when you use its services, including through apps that connect to it.

Third parties

Data leaves your devices only for the optional features above: to Supabase for Cloud Sync and Strava connections, and to Strava for uploads you turned on. Apple handles Sign in with Apple and Apple Health under Apple's own privacy policy. There are no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services in the app, and nothing is shared with data brokers.

How long data is kept and how to delete it

  • Delete your account in Settings → Cloud Sync → Delete Account. This immediately deletes all your cloud data, including your Strava connection, and revokes the app's Sign in with Apple access. Data on your devices stays until you delete it or the app.
  • Sign out keeps your cloud copy for when you sign in again.
  • Cloud data is otherwise kept for as long as your account exists.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, a copy in a portable format, correction, deletion, or restriction of processing, and you can object to processing or withdraw consent at any time (for example by signing out, disconnecting Strava, or changing Health access). Write to stefan.blos@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority.
